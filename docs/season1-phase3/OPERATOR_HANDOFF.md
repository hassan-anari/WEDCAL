## Failure handling and remaining activation blockers

Before activation, the owner must authorize the Production inspection, assess historical evidence gaps and expiring ingress, approve the completed coverage report and producer cutover, and separately authorize deployment/activation. Those are operational blockers, not invented pending numerical questions. Historical Epochs that cannot be reconstructed remain fail-closed for global unknowns; known beneficiaries remain scoped and auditable rather than silently validated.

A depleted reserve or unprovable historical entitlement has no automatic successful fallback. Preserve the case and report exact missing proof/funding; a new owner-approved disposition is necessary before final close. Original settled allocations are never rewritten. Since the one correction round is final after admission, a later discovered case remains unresolved and cannot trigger a second payment round without a separately approved policy change. These controls intentionally favor accountable unresolved status over unfunded promises.

No API fields/routes are added. Existing DLX balance now includes funded correction credits and holds/forfeitures; still reports claimable0 and never adds XP/Level. Preflight remains read-only and cannot grant activation authority.
